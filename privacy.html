Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

RCSM is a personal cloud backup and synchronization utility that uses Google Drive for file storage.

Files are stored in the user's own Google Drive account. Access is authorized through Google OAuth and used only for backup and synchronization.

RCSM does not sell personal data or share user files with advertisers.

Users can delete their stored files and revoke access at any time through their Google account.
